About BackpackDiaries

Your travels deserve to be remembered.

BackpackDiaries is a travel diary app built for adventurers, backpackers, and weekend wanderers. Record trips, pin destinations, attach photos, and build a living archive of every place you've ever been.

  • Log destinations with maps, dates, and personal notes
  • Build a visual timeline of your adventures year by year
  • Organize photos into beautiful country-based galleries
  • Write rich journal entries — even offline
What's inside

Everything a traveler needs

Six thoughtfully designed tools that turn scattered memories into a cohesive story of your life on the road.

Travel Journals

Write free-form diary entries with rich text, dates, mood tags, and location stamps for every day of your trip.

Memory Timeline

Scroll back through a chronological ribbon of all your journeys — filtered by year, season, or destination.

Photo Gallery

Attach photos to any entry and browse them in a stunning mosaic gallery organized by trip or country.

Destination Notes

Save quick notes, tips, local phrases, and must-do lists for every city or region you visit.

Favourite Places

Bookmark spots you love — restaurants, viewpoints, hidden gems — to share or revisit on your next adventure.

Offline Entries

No Wi-Fi? No problem. Write entries locally and everything syncs automatically when you're back online.
